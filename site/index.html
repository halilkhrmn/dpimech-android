What is it?

Some internet providers block sites and apps with DPI (deep packet inspection). ByeDPI gets around it by changing how the first packets of a connection look. DPIMech for Android runs ByeDPI on your phone, only for the apps you pick, with the same tested strategies as the desktop app.

Not a VPN service. Android's VPN feature is only used to catch the chosen apps' traffic on the phone. Every connection still goes straight to the site: nothing passes through someone else's server, your IP address is not hidden, and sites blocked by IP address stay blocked.

  • A three-step wizard: pick the sites, it tests your connection and turns the working setting on.
  • Whole phone for the sites commonly blocked in your country, or only the apps you pick.
  • Automatic strategy: on a new network it finds and remembers what works there.
  • Ready-made packs: Discord, YouTube, Roblox, X, Instagram, Wattpad — the pack also ticks the app.
  • Only the pack's sites get the bypass; everything else in the app is left alone.
  • Strategies shared with the desktop app, updated without a new release, plus a community list.
  • Encrypted DNS (DNS over HTTPS) for the bypassed apps, and an optional QUIC block so browsers and YouTube use TCP.
  • Live traffic, average ping of your sites and DNS counts on the home screen and in the notification.
  • Widgets (one per profile if you like), shortcuts that turn a profile on and open its app, Quick Settings tile.
  • No root, no Google services, no ads, no tracking. English, Türkçe, Русский, فارسی, العربية.

Is it safe?

  • All code is open. ByeDPI and hev-socks5-tunnel are built from source inside the APK; nothing executable is downloaded later.
  • Strategy lists are plain text, and every option is checked against an allowlist before ByeDPI starts.
  • The engine only listens on the phone itself (127.0.0.1).
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporters.
  • Android allows one VPN at a time, so another VPN or a VPN-based ad blocker cannot run together with it.
